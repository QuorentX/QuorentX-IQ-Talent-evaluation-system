# QuorentX IQ — Brand Guidelines (One-Pager)

**Product:** QuorentX IQ · **Parent:** QuorentX · **Tagline:** Smarter interviews, better hires

---

## 1. Name & meaning

| Element | Usage |
|--------|--------|
| **QuorentX** | Parent brand; always navy/ink |
| **IQ** | Product mark; always signal teal; never all-caps as “IQ PLATFORM” |
| **QuorentX IQ** | Full product name in sentence case |

**IQ meaning (use sparingly in copy):** Intelligence (AI-driven) **and** Interview Quotient (scored evaluation).

---

## 2. Logo lockup

**Primary (horizontal):** iQ pictorial mark + wordmark `QuorentX` (navy) + `IQ` (teal).

| Asset | Path | Use |
|-------|------|-----|
| Mark only | `/brand/iq-mark.svg` · `/logo-iq.png` | Hero, splash, large displays |
| Lockup | `/brand/logo-lockup.svg` | Headers, docs, decks |
| Lockup + tagline | `/brand/logo-lockup-tagline.svg` | Cover slides, email footers |
| Icon square 32/64/128/256 | `/brand/icon-square-*.svg` | Favicon, app icon, PWA |
| Icon circle 64 | `/brand/icon-circle-64.svg` | Avatar, social crop |

**Clear space:** ≥ height of the “i” stem on all sides.  
**Minimum size:** lockup ≥ 120px wide; icon ≥ 24px.  
**Do not:** stretch, recolor arbitrarily, add gradients/shadows, place on busy photos without a solid plate.

---

## 3. Color palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Primary blue | `#185FA5` | Links, trust UI, i-stem |
| Deep navy | `#042C53` | Headers, dark surfaces, Q-ring |
| Signal teal | `#0F6E56` | IQ accent, primary CTAs (sparingly) |
| Warm neutral | `#F1EFE8` | Page backgrounds |
| Ink | `#2C2C2A` | Body text |

**Rules:** Teal = accent only (CTAs, IQ letter, highlights). Prefer navy for large dark fields. No purple gradients; no heavy glow.

---

## 4. Typography

| Role | Weight | Notes |
|------|--------|--------|
| Headings | Medium **500** | Geometric/humanist sans (DM Sans) |
| Body | Regular **400** | Line-height ~1.6–1.7 |

**Avoid:** 700/900 weights, decorative serifs, all-caps headlines.

---

## 5. Voice & tone

**Be:** intelligent, precise, trustworthy, approachable — a smart colleague.  
**Avoid:** corporate-stiff jargon, startup hype (“revolutionary”, “disrupting”), black-box AI claims.

**Example:** “Evaluate candidates with structured scorecards and explainable scores.”  
**Not:** “Unleash next-gen AI that reads minds in interviews.”

---

## 6. Product promise (positioning)

Full-stack interview intelligence for mid-market to enterprise: scheduling, structured interviews, AI scoring, and hiring analytics — without enterprise-tool complexity or single-purpose shallowness.

**Buyers:** HR/TA teams and technical hiring managers.

---

## 7. Access model (product UX)

Company workspaces are **invite-only** via QuorentX. Candidates sign in with issued credentials. Public self-signup for orgs is not offered.
