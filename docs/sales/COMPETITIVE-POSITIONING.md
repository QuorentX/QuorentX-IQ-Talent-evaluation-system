# QuorentX IQ — Competitive Positioning (One-Pager / Sales Slide)

## Headline

**One platform for the full interview stack — explainable AI, mid-market fit, quality-of-hire proof.**

---

## The category problem

| Vendor type | What they do well | What customers still stitch |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| Scheduling-only | Calendars, rooms | Kits, scoring, analytics |
| Intelligence-only | Insights after the fact | Live workflow, candidate UX |
| Assessment-only | Tests | Structured live interviews |
| Heavy enterprise suites | Breadth | Speed, simplicity, cost |

**QuorentX IQ** unifies scheduling, structured interviews, AI scoring, and hiring analytics — without forcing a four-vendor stack or a 12-month implementation.

---

## Why us (differentiation)

1. **Unified full-stack** — All four layers in one product; less glue, clearer ownership.  
2. **Explainable, auditable AI** — Rubric-linked evidence, not black-box tone/face scoring. Built for rising legal scrutiny on AI hiring.  
3. **Mid-market sweet spot** — More capable than point tools; lighter than enterprise platforms that need armies of admins.  
4. **Quality-of-hire proof** — Analytics that aim to correlate interview scores to on-the-job performance over time — not only “faster time-to-hire.”  
5. **Candidate-side value** — Practice/mock mode and branded experience, not recruiter-only tooling.
