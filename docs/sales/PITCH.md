# QuorentX IQ — Pitch Paragraph

## Investor / sales conversation (primary)

QuorentX IQ is QuorentX’s AI-powered interview intelligence platform for mid-market and enterprise hiring teams who need to evaluate candidates faster and fairer without stitching together a scheduler, a scorecard tool, an assessment vendor, and a separate “AI insights” product. IQ — short for both Intelligence and Interview Quotient — unifies scheduling, structured interviews, explainable AI scoring, and hiring analytics in one stack. Unlike black-box hiring AI, every suggested score is tied to a rubric and evidence interviewers can audit and override. We sit in the mid-market sweet spot: deeper than single-purpose tools, lighter than heavy enterprise suites. Over time we prove quality of hire—not just speed—by correlating interview performance to on-the-job outcomes, while giving candidates a branded portal and practice mode that competitors treat as an afterthought. Access is provisioned by QuorentX for serious hiring organizations; the parent company already delivers intelligent data and AI solutions to enterprises worldwide.
